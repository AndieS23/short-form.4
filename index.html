Course Goals


1. Consider the role of rhetoric, technology, and ethics in acts of making.

While the art of design is the bridge to creativity you need to have the understaning of who you are designing for. You need to be able to know the usuage of each design platform and what you are using it to create. Design is all opinion based but when it comes to designing for someone you need outsiders opinons.

2. Leverage Design Principles.

Being able to use the correct tools and apply them to what you are trying to create makes your design more accountable. By using complementary colors, differnt fonts, layout space, and unique tools are great examples.

3. Engage with others in a workshop environment.

By including the daily workshops with classmates this helps evalutate your work and gives you an outside opinions rather than your close peers in your life. You get to learn simpler ways to do something that you found difficult or learn new tools that you wouldn't have thought of using.



4. Practice revision within and across media forms.

Revision is always key to everything that you create on your own espically when being presented to your peers. By changing your work by the littlest can make the biggest differnece even you thought it looks very good in the beginning.

5. Learn to present your work.

Presentation is key to any work aspect you put yourself in. Half of your life is presenting your work or your opinion on a topic. You need to learn how to connect with the audience and become confident on your topic of choice. Most important thing is to take the critasism you are given and use it as potential for your work.
